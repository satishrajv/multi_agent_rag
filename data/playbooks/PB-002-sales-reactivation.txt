Title: Reactivation Strategy for Dormant Deals

Category: sales
Success Rate: 65%
Number of Cases: 18

When to Use:
- No activity in 7+ days
- Opportunity in any stage
- Last touchpoint was from prospect (not us)
- Deal hasn't progressed in 2+ weeks

Recommended Actions:
1. Review last touchpoint and identify blocker
   - What was discussed?
   - What action item was pending?
   - Who dropped the ball?
   - What changed in their environment?

2. Send a 2-option value email (not "just checking in")
   - Option A: Specific value proposition
   - Option B: Alternative approach
   - Force a decision/response
   - Example: "Would you prefer X or Y approach?"

3. Offer specific business insights or competitive intelligence
   - Industry trends relevant to them
   - Competitor movements
   - Regulatory changes
   - Market opportunities

4. Propose concrete next step with calendar invite
   - Don't ask "when are you free?"
   - Propose 2-3 specific time slots
   - Include clear agenda
   - Set duration (keep it short, 20-30 min)

5. If no response in 3 days, loop in their manager
   - Professional escalation
   - New perspective
   - Higher-level conversation
   - Avoid being pushy

Success Factors:
- Providing value in every outreach (not just asking for updates)
- Specific calendar invites get 3x more responses than "let me know"
- Industry insights increase engagement by 42%
- 2-option emails have 28% higher response rate
- Escalation (when done right) closes 23% of dormant deals

Common Pitfalls:
- Generic "just checking in" emails
- Not offering tangible value
- Waiting too long before re-engaging (sweet spot is 7-10 days)
- Being too aggressive with follow-ups
- Not acknowledging the gap in communication

Email Templates:

Template 1 - Value-Based Reactivation:
Subject: 2 quick ideas for [Company]

Hi [Name],

I know things get busy. I wanted to share two quick thoughts that might be relevant:

Option A: [Specific insight about their industry/challenge]
Option B: [Alternative approach we haven't discussed]

Which resonates more with your current priorities?

Happy to jump on a brief call Tuesday 2pm or Thursday 10am to explore further.

Best,
[Your name]

Template 2 - Competitive Intelligence:
Subject: [Competitor] just announced [relevant news]

Hi [Name],

Saw that [Competitor] just [specific action]. This could impact [their business area].

Based on our earlier conversations, this might accelerate your timeline for [initiative].

Would a 20-min call this week be helpful to discuss implications?

Best,
[Your name]

Expected Results:
- 30-40% response rate within 3 days
- 20-25% convert to active pipeline again
- 65% overall success rate for re-engaged deals

Timeline:
- Day 1: Research and prepare value content
- Day 2: Send reactivation email
- Day 5: Follow-up if no response
- Day 8: Manager escalation if still no response
- Day 10: Mark as closed-lost if no engagement
