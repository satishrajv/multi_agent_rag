Title: Red to Green Project Recovery

Category: delivery
Success Rate: 85%
Number of Cases: 14

When to Use:
- Projects in Red status
- Less than 7 days to deadline
- Progress significantly behind schedule
- Multiple overdue tasks

Recommended Actions:
1. Start 15-minute daily huddle with delivery manager
   - Every morning at same time
   - Review yesterday's progress
   - Identify today's top 3 priorities
   - Clear blockers immediately
   - No status updates - action-oriented only

2. Identify top 3 critical blockers
   - What's preventing progress?
   - Client blockers vs team blockers vs technical blockers
   - Severity and impact assessment
   - Owner assignment for each blocker

3. Escalate client response gaps to account/sales team
   - Client not responding? Don't wait
   - Sales team has different leverage
   - Usually gets response within 24 hours
   - Be specific about what you need

4. Re-scope non-essential items to Phase 2
   - Be honest about what's achievable
   - Protect core functionality
   - Document what's moving to Phase 2
   - Get written agreement from client

5. Get written confirmation on revised scope within 24 hours
   - Email summary of agreements
   - Clear acceptance criteria
   - New timeline
   - Client sign-off required

Success Factors:
- Daily huddles have 85% success rate in Red → Green transitions
- Scope re-alignment is critical (trying to do everything fails)
- Client escalation by sales team speeds up responses by 3x
- Getting written confirmation prevents scope creep
- Manager involvement shows seriousness

Why This Works:
- Daily visibility prevents surprises
- Scope reduction is often necessary for success
- Sales team has different relationship with client
- Clear priorities focus the team
- Written agreements protect everyone

Common Pitfalls:
- Trying to recover without rescoping (95% fail rate)
- Not involving sales team for client escalation soon enough
- Weekly check-ins instead of daily (too slow for Red projects)
- Not getting written confirmation (leads to disputes)
- Focusing on status updates instead of actions

Daily Huddle Structure (15 minutes):
- Minute 1-3: Yesterday's completions
- Minute 4-8: Today's top 3 priorities
- Minute 9-13: Blockers and owner assignments
- Minute 14-15: Quick decisions/escalations

Re-scoping Conversation Guide:
1. Acknowledge the situation honestly
2. Present current reality (progress vs deadline)
3. Propose core vs nice-to-have split
4. Explain Phase 2 plan
5. Get agreement and sign-off

Client Escalation Template:
Subject: Urgent: Need [specific item] for [Project] by [date]

Hi [Sales Rep],

We need your help with [Project Name]. We're blocked on [specific blocker]
and need [specific action] from [client stakeholder] by [date].

Context: [1-2 sentences on impact]
What we need: [specific, actionable request]
Deadline: [specific date/time]
Impact if missed: [clear consequence]

Can you help escalate this? Happy to jump on a call to discuss.

Thanks,
[Your name]

Expected Timeline:
- Day 1: Start daily huddles, identify blockers
- Day 2: Escalate client issues, propose re-scoping
- Day 3: Get written agreement on new scope
- Days 4-7: Execute with daily huddles
- Result: Green status within 5-7 days

Metrics to Track:
- Number of blockers cleared per day
- Client response time
- Scope reduction percentage
- Team velocity improvement
- Time to green status
